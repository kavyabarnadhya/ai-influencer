## Operating rules
- 17 posts: 13 stills-to-slideshow reels and four five-slide carousels. Three reels and one carousel each week, plus one extra reel on day 29. Day 30 is measurement only.
- Make five persona-consistent stills for a scheduled post. Carousel: five slides. Reel: 1080 x 1920 Ken Burns/slideshow from the stills. Publish only the scheduled format, not identical back-to-back duplicates. Put the first-line hook over the opening still and draft a matching caption. Check face, hands, garment details, hook clearance and final export.
- Persona voice: casual, coffee-obsessed, outfit-led Indian city life. Her existing profile says Delhi. Do not claim a real Bangalore visit or change her Delhi backstory without a separate decision. Do not invent lived events, actual weather or locations.
- Instagram music is a posting-side decision, not a generated asset. AI-content label ON for every post when publishing. No boosts, subscriptions, paid credits or new paid tools. Producing assets does not mean they have been posted.
- The first four posts (week 1) require the user's review and approval of both final visual and caption before publication. Auto-posting after week 1 is conditional on the user's separate approval that the calibration worked. Instagram browser access is currently paused after an automated-behavior warning; coordinate with the posting side before assuming any post can go live.

## Measurement and decisions
- At launch record account follower count and existing carousel/reel performance as pre-test baselines. The existing carousel is https://www.instagram.com/heyyananyaaa/p/DdvNObelrlu/ . Do not count earlier posts as test wins.
- At 48 hours after each live post, log date, format, theme, hook, plays/views, unique reach, saves, shares, profile visits, follows and account follower count. Compare reels against reels and carousels against carousels. If a metric is not available, mark it unavailable rather than estimating it.
- At each weekly checkpoint make a keep/kill/adjust call using the rules above. Reach low: change first frame/hook. Reach healthy but follows low: improve profile promise and serial payoff. After two tested variations with no lift, stop scaling the slideshow-reel format.
- Day-30 proof gate: at least two test reels with 500+ plays AND the account at approximately 200 followers with positive net growth. If met, repeat the best format organically; it is not approval to spend. If not, report which side of the gate failed and diagnose hook vs follow conversion. Any D29 reel needs its mature 48-hour read after D30.
